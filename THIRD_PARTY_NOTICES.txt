Poster — third-party components

Full Maker uses dynamically linked Qt / PySide6 6.12.0 and Qt WebEngine.
The used Qt Widgets, Qt Core, Qt GUI and Qt WebEngine interfaces are available
under LGPLv3 (with GPL/commercial alternatives). Qt is not modified by Poster.
License texts are supplied in licenses/qt in the Full distribution. The dynamic
libraries remain replaceable; extract the entire portable directory together.
Qt and PySide source/distribution information:
https://code.qt.io/
https://github.com/pyside/pyside-setup/tree/v6.12.0
https://www.qt.io/licensing/open-source-lgpl-obligations

Qt WebEngine includes Chromium and its third-party components, with their own
BSD/MIT/other notices and attributions in the WebEngine resources. Preserve those
resources when copying the portable application. Do not remove WebEngine resources
or replace individual DLL files with incompatible versions.
